    Hi! Welcome to my page! I'm Sophia

    1. What do I do?

      I build and position complex engineering products, especially swarm robotics, so they can be used by everyone globally.

      Engineering projects, mostly swarm robotics one. I enjoy creating and coming up with ideas. It is especially important to me, that the robots I develop are potentially usable by everyone all around the world.

    2. What am I good at?

      Short answer:

      Giving a product a global perspective and selling that

      Long answer:

      I get excited quickly and am able to find parallels in all aspects in life. As a result, I often draw inspiration from fields that superficially have nothing to do with each other, but fundamentally work the same.

      I like having a broad base of knowledge and experiences in different areas of knowledge, every project is part of the cultural context, and every experience opens a new perspective. Thus, it all depends not only on the people developing it, but also on how the user is able to use it/ perceive it.

      All of these are complex socioeconomic and technical connections. Naturally, I specialize in the field of collaborative and swarm robotic systems. This area connects all my interests. I aim to build a swarm of robots for complex constructions and bring this technology into practical, accessible, and global use.

    3. Where am I heading?

      My vision of that robotic swarm does not exist yet, so it has to be developed. To make it a reality, I have trained a variety of skills, from leadership to technical development to marketing. I have deducted that the role I am most suited for is actually the selling of the idea and products. Thus, I want to learn more about this and do it professionally.
